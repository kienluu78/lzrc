02 Principles

restraint.

The same four rules on every surface: public page, private pilot, teaching room.

01

Verify, don't infer

No source in the graph, no claim in the answer. Fluency is free; being right is the product.

02

A human at the door

High-severity actions wait for a logged approval. Write access stays narrower than conversation.

03

Memory stays ours

Weights get replaced. The institutional record does not live inside a vendor's chat log.

04

Models interchangeable

Architecture outlasts any single model. Continuity is the moat, not a brand of weights.
